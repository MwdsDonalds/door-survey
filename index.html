Complimentary Door Condition Survey

Tap a check to cycle: blank → G Green = Good → Y Yellow = Watch → P Pink = Fix now → blank

Visual condition survey only. This is not a code inspection, certification, or NFPA 80 inspection report. Midwest D-Vision Solutions recommends an annual fire door assembly inspection by a qualified inspector.

Check legend and what to quote

1 Closes & latches → closer, latch, strike, adjustment
2 Gaps → hinges, door adjustment, door replacement
3 Labels → relabel or door/frame replacement
4 Holes / damage / mods → protection plate, approved filler, replacement
5 Propped / wedged → magnetic hold-open or closer-holder tied to the fire alarm
6 Panic / exit hardware → exit device or rebuild kit
7 Coordinator / astragal / seals → coordinator, astragal, gasketing
Field tip: about 60–90 seconds per door. Priority areas: stair doors, corridor fire doors, exit doors with panic hardware, main entrances, and health care smoke and cross-corridor doors.
